Haidr Inbox: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Haidr Inbox ("the app") is a private tool used only by the Instagram creator account @haidr.mp4 to manage its own Instagram direct messages and comments. It is not offered to the public and no one else can log in to it.

What data the app accesses

  • Direct messages sent to @haidr.mp4 (message text, time sent, the sender's Instagram-scoped ID and username).
  • Comments left on @haidr.mp4's posts and reels (comment text, time, commenter's username).
  • Basic profile information and post statistics of @haidr.mp4.

How the data is used

  • To sort incoming messages into simple categories (for example: questions about clipping, collaboration requests, brand enquiries, spam) so the account owner can reply faster.
  • To draft suggested replies, which the account owner reviews before sending.
  • To send one private reply with a requested link (for example our free Discord community) when someone comments a keyword such as "DISCORD" on a post.

We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone. We do not use it for advertising. We do not send unsolicited messages; the app only replies to people who message or comment first, within Instagram's messaging rules.

Where the data is stored

Data is stored only on the account owner's personal computer. It is not uploaded to any third-party database. Messages are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted automatically.

Deleting your data

To have any data about you deleted, follow the steps on our data deletion page, or send a direct message to @haidr.mp4 on Instagram with the word "DELETE". We will delete all stored messages and comments from you within 7 days and confirm by reply.

Contact

Questions about this policy: message @haidr.mp4 on Instagram or email haidrmedia@gmail.com.
