Haidr Inbox: Data Deletion Instructions

Haidr Inbox only stores messages and comments sent to the Instagram account @haidr.mp4, on the owner's own computer.

To delete all data the app holds about you:

  1. Send a direct message to @haidr.mp4 on Instagram with the word DELETE, or email haidrmedia@gmail.com with your Instagram username.
  2. All stored messages and comments from your account are deleted within 7 days.
  3. You will get a reply confirming the deletion.

Messages are also deleted automatically after 90 days.

See the full privacy policy.
